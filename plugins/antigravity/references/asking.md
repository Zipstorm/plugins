# Asking the recruiter

- Use an obvious reading/default and state it; do not turn it into a question.
- Ask with your own question tool where this chat offers one; otherwise use a short numbered list.
  Accept option numbers/text and free text. Ask at most three independent questions
  per turn; ask dependent questions after their prerequisite answer.
- Ask only unresolved choices. Do not re-confirm explicit requirements or
  corrections. A proposed answer may include a short reason.
- Ask recurring choices once per conversation. Offer to remember them only when
  the client supports [persistent memory](memory.md).
- For requested drafts, start the draft and mark missing facts in it, such as
  “[salary range: to confirm].” Otherwise ask the next useful question.
- Refusals state the reason and continue valid work; do not ask whether a forbidden
  request was intentional. Keep protocol details out and never request credentials,
  tokens, tenant IDs or custom URLs.
