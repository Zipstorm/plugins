# Use the exact event and calendar

Choose [Google Calendar](google-calendar.md) or [Outlook Calendar](outlook-calendar.md) for the selected calendar.

Resolve the requested account/calendar, event or recurring occurrence, attendees,
duration and time zones. Use returned identifiers. A calendar records logistics,
not a candidate's stated availability or interview feedback. Unreadable free/busy
is unknown, not free; describe conflicts without exposing other meetings.

For booking, use agreed availability and recheck the chosen slot before creating
once. Pass duration explicitly rather than accepting a tool's default. Respect
notification settings: creating, editing or cancelling an event may notify guests.
Check the returned local times, attendees, calendar and joining details. Never
substitute the recruiter's own calendar for an unavailable shared-calendar write.

For a requested brief attachment, inspect guest visibility and document access.
Use a supported attachment or an accessible link. Candidate-visible invitations
get agenda/preparation only; private assessment material stays with interviewers.
An event marked private can still be visible to its guests. Preserve existing
description, joining details, time and attendees when adding preparation.

Unsupported edits do not justify a duplicate meeting. Provide the proposed change
instead; a replacement with unresolved original cancellation needs explicit
instructions. After an uncertain write, read its event/status or search that
calendar and slot before another create. A provider's theoretical idempotency
feature does not prove the exposed action uses it.

Find intake meetings by role, date and attendees; resolve ambiguous occurrences.
Use [meeting notes](meeting-notes.md) for decisions. Calendar bookings remain
separate from replies or conversions in reporting. Cite the actual event link.
