# Gmail

Gmail is where the recruiter's own messages leave and where candidates answer.
SeekOut finds the people, holds their addresses and keeps each one's status in
the role's workspace; Gmail sends what was written for them and holds the
thread that follows. Neither sees the other, so the conversation carries the
result across.

It comes in at three points of a role's work:

- **Before outreach,** to check whether the recruiter already wrote to someone
  the workspace shows as not contacted.
- **During outreach,** to save a draft or send a message written for a
  workspace's candidate, to the address SeekOut returned. A confirmed send is
  what marks that candidate Contacted in the workspace.
- **After outreach,** to find replies from the people the workspace shows as
  Contacted. A reply read here is what sets Replied, Interested or Not
  interested there.

It also carries mail that is not candidate outreach: hiring-manager updates,
booking links, reference requests and rejections.

Search by address as well as name, read a thread through its latest message,
and reply inside it. A search count is an estimate and a hit is not a read.
Use the connected account; a mailbox link does not switch accounts. The rules
for drafts, sends and uncertain results are in [mail work](mail.md).

Bring what it shows back to the role as the
[workspace handoff](workspace-context.md) says.
