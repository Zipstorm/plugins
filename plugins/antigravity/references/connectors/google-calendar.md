# Google Calendar

Google Calendar is where a meeting about a role becomes real. SeekOut knows the role,
its candidates and where each one stands; the calendar knows who is free and
holds the event. A booking is the point where an interested candidate becomes
someone the team is meeting, so it is also a point where the workspace is
brought up to date.

It comes in at these points of a role's work:

- **At intake,** to book the meeting with the hiring manager that the role's
  overview is built from.
- **After an interested reply,** to book a screen or interview with a
  workspace's candidate. Once the event is confirmed, that candidate's status
  in the workspace is updated.
- **Before a meeting,** to read the day's events and put a private prep beside
  each, written from the role's workspace and the people's recent messages.
- **After interviews,** to book the debrief.

The calendar holds logistics only. It does not hold a candidate's stated
availability, their interest or any feedback, and SeekOut does not keep the
event link, so give the link in the conversation.

Use the Google account and calendar the recruiter means; the primary calendar
is not always the recruiting one. For a recurring meeting, tell the series from
the one occurrence being changed. An event link and a video link are different
things.

Someone outside the organization picks their own time on an appointment
schedule: in Calendar, Create, Appointment schedule, set the length and hours,
then Share and copy the link. A booking made there lands on the recruiter's
calendar like any other event.

A private prep appointment is an event with no guests, visibility private and
shown as free.

The rules for reading free time, booking once and uncertain writes are in
[calendar work](calendar.md).

Bring what it shows back to the role as the
[workspace handoff](workspace-context.md) says.
