# Google Calendar

Use for intake meetings, candidate interviews and attaching preparation. Follow
[calendar work](calendar.md); select the intended Google account and calendar
rather than assuming its primary calendar is the recruiting calendar.

Resolve an existing event by its returned calendar/event identity. For recurring
meetings, distinguish the series from the occurrence being changed. Confirm time
zones, guest notifications and conferencing fields in the available action.
An event link and a video-joining link serve different purposes. Attach a brief
only through a supported attachment or an accessible document link.

Carry the calendar, event/occurrence ID and returned event link into the
[workspace handoff](workspace-context.md). Keep candidate-facing preparation in
the invitation and private evaluation material in the interviewer brief.
