# Google Drive

Use for role briefs, JDs, approved compensation bands, interview kits and hiring
spreadsheets. Follow [document work](documents.md). Start from the role's supplied
file or folder; distinguish files with the same title by returned identity,
location, owner and modified date.

A Drive search result may identify a document without exposing its body. Use the
available document or spreadsheet reader for content. For Sheets, identify the
spreadsheet, tab and bounded range; a title or visible tab alone is insufficient.
Copy a requested template rather than editing the template itself.

Carry file/folder IDs and returned links into the
[workspace handoff](workspace-context.md). Saving a file does not grant an
interviewer access. Keep permissions separate from attaching its link to a meeting.
