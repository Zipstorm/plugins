# Work with the chosen mailbox

Choose [Gmail](gmail.md) or [Outlook Email](outlook-email.md) when that is the selected mailbox.

Draft from the supplied facts immediately. Saving a mailbox draft and sending
are separate requested actions. A send needs an available send action and its
required confirmation; consent scopes alone do not prove that action exists.
Keep sender, recipients, subject, body and attachments explicit. Use the exact
shared/delegated mailbox requested, never silently switch to a personal account.

For prior contact or follow-up, resolve people by address/profile link as well
as name. Read the relevant thread through its latest message and keep replies
in that thread. Message and thread IDs are distinct. Exclude replies, bounces,
opt-outs and active sequences as outreach requires; unavailable reads mean
suppression was not checked. Do not widen to unrelated mail.

Use supported sender/recipient, subject and date filters. Incomplete broad
searches can be narrowed into time windows. Search estimates are not exact counts.
For analytics, retain the mailbox, folders, window and thread-selection method;
show numerator/denominator and separate automatic replies. Mail and CRM sequence
counts can overlap, so do not add them.

Learn writing style only when requested or needed: sample a small relevant set,
keep patterns rather than personal details, and do not block a draft on it.
A hiring-manager request is proposed criteria until accepted under
[criteria guidance](../criteria.md).

Report the outcome precisely: text drafted, mailbox draft saved, provider-confirmed
send, explicit failure or unknown. Sent does not mean delivered. After an unknown
send, inspect the original action or Sent mail by recipient, subject and attempt
time; never resend while uncertain. If a draft request unexpectedly sends, report
what happened without repeating or trying to undo it. Follow
[external actions](../external-actions.md).
