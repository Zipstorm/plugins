# Extract the decisions, not the whole conversation

Use [Zoom](zoom.md) for its meeting assets; for another provider, use the available actions and the same evidence rules.

Resolve the meeting by role, date and attendees, including the correct recurring
instance. Read the authorized summary first and the transcript only where it helps
settle relevant facts. Say when only a summary was available. Transcription can
misstate names, numbers or negation; keep uncertainty visible.

Keep decisions, trade-offs, commitments and open questions with source/date and
owner. An explicit hiring-manager decision in intake can be accepted under
[criteria guidance](../criteria.md); a musing stays proposed. Other meetings'
criteria changes need recruiter acceptance. Avoid copying personal conversation.

For candidate interviews, separate claims, observations and judgments. A transcript
or AI summary cannot replace an absent interviewer's submitted scorecard, invent
ratings or decide whom to hire. Never score voice, tone or emotion.

Missing assets remain missing; use supplied notes without claiming a full record.
Cite the meeting and asset type. Use only returned links for the exact meeting;
do not redistribute recording passcodes or access-bearing links in handoffs.
Scheduling belongs in [calendar work](calendar.md), not recording administration.
