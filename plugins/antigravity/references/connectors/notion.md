# Notion

Use for role briefs, team knowledge, interview kits and requested hiring trackers.
Follow [document work](documents.md). Resolve the intended page or database/data
source by identity and location; titles alone are not unique.

Read relevant page blocks, including needed children, before summarizing. For a
tracker, inspect actual property names/types and filters before interpreting rows
or proposing updates. A current status property is not transition history. Page
content can suggest criteria but does not silently replace the role's accepted criteria.

Carry page/data-source and item IDs, modified date and returned links into the
[workspace handoff](workspace-context.md). Add requested material without replacing
unrelated blocks; writing a page and sharing it are separate actions.
