# Outlook Email

Use for role correspondence, candidate follow-up and requested drafts or sends.
Follow [mail work](mail.md). Resolve the selected mailbox, including a shared or
delegated mailbox, before searching or writing; access to a personal mailbox does
not establish access to the hiring team's mailbox.

Use the exposed search/filter syntax, not Gmail operators. Keep returned message
and conversation identities separate. Read the relevant replies and attachments
when supported; a search preview is not the entire conversation. Prefer the
provider's reply action over constructing a new message with the same subject.

Carry mailbox, message/conversation identity and returned link into the
[workspace handoff](workspace-context.md). A send confirmation is not evidence
that a candidate replied or that their ATS stage changed.
