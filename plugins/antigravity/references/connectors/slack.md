# Slack

Use for hiring-manager discussions, role decisions and requested team handoffs.
Follow [team chat](team-chat.md). Bind the role to the intended Slack workspace,
channel and thread; Slack's workspace is distinct from the SeekOut workspace.

Search the supplied channel/thread and relevant date window. Read the parent
and needed replies before summarizing a decision. A name match alone is not a
verified channel, person or mention. Keep the returned thread permalink with the
decision, and distinguish a hiring-manager commitment from discussion or reactions.

For a requested post, use the exact destination and supported mention mechanism.
Carry channel/thread identity and permalink into the
[workspace handoff](workspace-context.md). A posted request does not establish
that the recipient accepted or completed it.
