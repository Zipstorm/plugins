# Zoom

Zoom holds what was said in a meeting about a role: its summary, transcript
and recording. SeekOut holds the role's requirements and candidates; a meeting
here is evidence for changing the first and for assessing the second.

It comes in at two points of a role's work:

- **After intake,** to pull the hiring manager's decisions into the role's
  overview. An explicit decision can be accepted; a musing stays proposed.
- **After an interview,** to support the debrief with what the candidate
  actually said. It never replaces an interviewer's own feedback, gives no
  rating and decides nothing.

A meeting here never sets a candidate's status; the recruiter's decision after
the debrief does. Booking or moving the meeting is calendar work.

Settle the exact occurrence by date and participants; a recurring meeting's
name is not enough. Read the summary first and only the parts of the transcript
that settle a fact, and say which you had. Never pass on a passcode or a
recording link that grants access. The rules for evidence are in
[meeting notes](meeting-notes.md).

Bring what it shows back to the role as the
[workspace handoff](workspace-context.md) says.
