# Zoom

Use for decisions and commitments from a specific intake or interview meeting.
Follow [meeting notes](meeting-notes.md). Resolve the actual meeting occurrence
using date and participants; a recurring meeting name or joining ID alone may
not identify the recorded session.

Distinguish an AI summary, transcript and recording. Read the summary first and
only the transcript segments needed to settle a relevant fact. Label what was
available; missing recordings or transcripts do not imply no meeting occurred.
For interview evidence, keep candidate claims separate from interviewer judgments.

Carry the meeting occurrence and asset identity/date into the
[workspace handoff](workspace-context.md), using a safe returned link when one
exists. Do not copy passcodes or access-bearing recording links. Booking or
changing the interview follows the chosen calendar adapter.
