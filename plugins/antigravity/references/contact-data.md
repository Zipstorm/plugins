# Contact data

- Use contact details from a tool result here or a recruiter-approved source;
  never guess addresses from names/domains. Match contact and profile identity;
  surface conflicting sources.
- A returned contact/public profile establishes neither permission nor application
  state. Shared employment is not a relationship/reference. Referee contacts must
  be supplied, never looked up ([safeguards](us-recruiting-safeguards.md), section 5).
- Opt-outs persist across addresses/providers. Before re-engagement, check prior
  contact, ownership and suppression; a missing field is not consent. No mass
  reactivation of old candidates.
- Use details for their retrieved purpose; omit from unnecessary summaries and
  wide circulation. Keep them out of files/memory and follow approved retention,
  not invented deadlines. Talent pools need a named anticipated need and freshness
  review, not indefinite archives or automatic nurturing.
- No commit-address harvesting, private-source scraping, hidden endpoints,
  anti-detection, private identity investigations, employee broadcasts or covert
  employer/reference contact. Respect provider/channel controls and terms; never
  request, retain or expose credentials.
