# Remembering the recruiter

Keep standing preferences separate from role-specific decisions.

## Where it lives

SeekOut stores outreach destination, report storage and job-to-workspace links;
role work stays in its [workspace](project.md), and how the recruiter works
across roles in their [work log](work-log.md). Read these settings and change
them only as requested. Other choices stay in conversation unless the client
supports persistent memory and the recruiter wants them kept. Without it, say
once at hand-off that those choices will need asking again.

## Read first, once

Read relevant standing choices, SeekOut settings and role context. Match a role
by title and market, never title alone; ask which req only if it affects work.
Do not import another role's locations, companies or figures; the one exception
is a [default criterion](work-log.md#default-criteria) the recruiter saved for
every role.

Latest recruiter instructions override remembered choices, which override
built-in behaviour. Briefly name choices that shaped the work. Store decisions, not
results; only a watch keeps a baseline, and each run remeasures it.

## What to keep

One dated line per fact, with source and role where relevant:

- **chose:** recruiter-selected settings and outputs.
- **allowed, declined:** systems and the scope of their use.
- **learned:** system structure, field/stage mappings and role identifiers.
- **said:** terminology, time zone and recurring job-related constraints.
- **corrected:** an explicit correction.
- **decided:** role decisions and questions still open for the hiring manager.
- **watching, baseline:** [watch](watches.md), schedule and scoped measurements,
  replaced after each successful run.

## When to write

Record explicit standing choices, corrections, decisions and created watches;
never guesses or personal opinions. Replace changed lines without disturbing
others, and acknowledge the saved choice once. “Forget that” removes the line;
“What do you remember?” shows the stored lines.

## Never keep

Candidate identities/profile keys/links or other person data; contact details;
salary history; mail, chat, notes or transcript text; credentials/token-bearing
links; raw outputs; anything the client's memory rules exclude. Keep a decision,
not the message that contained it.
