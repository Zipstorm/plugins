# Research and academic signals

Read when an ask depends on research output: researcher, professor, scholar,
PhD, postdoc, faculty, papers, publications, first-author, citations, h-index,
a named conference or journal, or patent inventor. These are signals to carry on
a public-profile search; the role, employer and place stay as the sourcing
skill's query reference builds them.

## Carrying the signals

| Ask | On the search |
| --- | --- |
| A PhD or another degree | The degrees filter |
| "Stanford PhD", a degree-granting school | The schools filter |
| A field, sub-field or topic ("computer vision") | Quoted term outside the title clause; one level per topic, the narrowest the recruiter named |
| A named conference or journal | Its common name and full name as quoted alternatives (`"NeurIPS" OR "Neural Information Processing Systems"`), in `publications:` when the ask is about publishing there |
| Patents | `patents:` with the topic |
| "Researchers at Stanford" (affiliation at publication time) | A check criterion judged on evidence; the schools filter only when they mean the degree |
| h-index, citations, paper or first-author counts | Check criteria with the recruiter's stated number, never above it |

"First-author only" with no number means at least one first-author
paper; say so.

## Examples

| Ask | Search |
| --- | --- |
| PhD researchers in NLP now in industry | `cur_title:("Research Scientist" OR "Applied Scientist") AND "natural language processing"`, degrees PhD |
| Patent-heavy battery researchers in Germany | Europe, Germany, `patents:("battery")`; patent count judged on evidence |

## Evidence

Cite what each profile shows: publications and patents with title and year,
degrees and schools, and current title, employer and location. When the
evidence for an asked constraint is missing (asked for patent-heavy, no patents
shown), say so rather than citing papers instead. Citation counts on a profile
are a snapshot, not live.

Relaxing a thin or empty pool is decided in the sourcing skill's refine
reference.
