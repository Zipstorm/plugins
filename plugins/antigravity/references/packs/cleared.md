# Defence, clearance and federal roles

Keep the requested function, level and employer tense. Defence-sector experience,
federal customers and a security clearance are different conditions; none proves
the others. A federal account executive is not an SDR, and a leadership request
must not silently widen to individual contributors.

For any listed clearance, use the supported generic clearance requirement.
For a named level, use this organization's offered clearance-level power filter
and distinguish declared from inferred signals when available. Never combine
the generic flag with a level filter. Without level support, a generic flag
plus relevant headline terms can produce an explicitly approximate pool;
verify the actual level, currency and eligibility separately. Employer, title
or location alone never confirms clearance. Do not promise reactivation.

For a defence-contractor pool, use an offered company filter or a sourced,
resolved employer list; disclose coverage. Preserve recruiter-named employers
and distinguish a federal business unit from its whole parent company.
Do not inject a familiar contractor list into unrelated searches.

Use [geographic guidance](location-and-indexes.md) for the requested DC-area
scope and [employer semantics](filter-semantics.md) for current versus past work.
Keep a rare cleared pool small when that is what the criteria support; only
propose broader scope through a labelled what-if.
