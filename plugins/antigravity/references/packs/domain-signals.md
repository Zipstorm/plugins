# Domain signals pack

Optional vocabulary for technical-domain searches/evidence. These are search
leads, not prestige ratings or proof of candidate fit. Resolve employer names
with company discovery/power filters and inspect the matched entity. Institutions
may be schools or affiliations; venues/topics are quoted public-profile terms
([academic](academic.md), [github](github.md)). Lists are examples, not complete markets.
