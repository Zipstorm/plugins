# Japan market pack

Read for roles based in Japan or that need Japanese: Japanese role
and GenAI vocabulary, language markers, mixed-script booleans.
Broadening procedure is in the sourcing skill's refine reference; the regions
are in the [location-and-indexes](location-and-indexes.md) pack.

## Language markers

- バイリンガル (bilingual), 日英バイリンガル (Japanese-English bilingual),
  日本語ネイティブ (native Japanese speaker).
- JLPT (日本語能力試験): levels N1 (highest) to N5. The required level is the
  recruiter's.

## Mixed-script boolean

Bilingual profiles mix scripts, so search both at once:

```text
(LLM OR "generative AI" OR "大規模言語モデル" OR 生成AI OR "プロンプトエンジニアリング")
```
