# Nursing

Search public profiles with the requested nursing role, unit, employers and
geography. Keep nurse types distinct: RN, LPN/LVN, NP and CRNA are not interchangeable.
Pair full titles with abbreviations; put specialty/unit terms beside the title,
for example `cur_title:("Registered Nurse" OR "RN") AND ("NICU" OR "Neonatal Intensive Care")`.
CRNAs are nurses; anesthesiologists use the [physician recipe](healthcare.md).

Use supported degree/certification filters only when required. A named hospital
is an employer; a hospital category needs a verified list or supported filter.
A licence state, compact status or willingness to travel does not establish
residence. Keep these as explicit verification conditions when the source
cannot enforce them; never invent a public-search licence filter.

Cite the profile's actual title, unit, employer and credentials. Preserve
self-reported post-nominals without claiming current licence verification.
Missing or truncated evidence is unknown, not a failed licence. Never invent
licence numbers or infer eligibility from the title. Refer current licence
status to the appropriate authoritative source.
