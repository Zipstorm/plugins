# A role's work

Keep each role's searches, saved people and reports in its SeekOut workspace.
Use the active workspace only if it is that role's; otherwise create the role's.
Read existing work first; make outside copies only when requested.

## What the role's work holds

A role is planned in its requirement trade-offs report ([plan a role](report-building.md)).
Its brief and [criteria](criteria.md#who-writes-them) are shared; other parts
have one owner:

- **Brief:** role summary, req, criteria/screen questions, open questions and
  decision owners, in the role's trade-offs report; intake writes it when work
  starts there.
- **Decisions:** the recording skill adds dated reasons to the report's next version.
- **Sourcing:** sourcing owns targets, off-limits employers and searches. Other
  skills hand it relevant manager, reply or decline findings.
- **Evidence:** reporting owns scoped measurements and measured trade-offs.
- **People:** pipeline management owns workspace candidates.
- **Reports:** reporting owns market and recruiting-performance reports.

## Where reports are kept

Follow the recruiter's storage setting. SeekOut is the default: save each
version in the role's workspace (an unnamed workspace uses the active one).
For local files or personal artifacts, save the content there first, then save
its filename or title/link to SeekOut's listing card. Without that location,
SeekOut refuses the save. Name where the version actually exists.

## People

Save only people SeekOut returned. Match externally found people first; an
ambiguous/unmatched person stays in conversation with the limitation explained.
Saved people begin shortlisted/not contacted. Read outreach status; do not
claim to set it. The recruiter records outreach/stage changes in SeekOut or
ATS. Keep requested notes in conversation or ATS and say if nothing was recorded
in SeekOut.

## Rules

- Never mix roles or borrow criteria, people or evidence without a request.
- Append dated decisions/evidence; mark superseded evidence stale. Save a
  report's next version rather than overwriting history.
- Person records contain only name, returned profile key/link, current title,
  employer and location; no contact data, credentials, raw output or salary history.
- Name the part changed. On failed saves, preserve the content and say where it remains.

## Connected work

Use the [workspace handoff](connectors/workspace-context.md) for external
source identities, drafts, events and scheduled tasks. Keep a conversation handoff
distinct from a confirmed durable workspace link.
