# US recruiting safeguards

For US roles/candidates: selection, pay, ads, offers, checks, work authorisation,
accommodations, AI, recordings and records. Section numbers are stable references.

## 1. Status and applicability

Baseline checked 2026-09-20, not legal certification. Follow stricter company
policy and recheck official sources/effective dates for the actual use; distinguish
law, proposals, guidance and enforcement. Applicability depends on work/remote
locations, employer size/footprint, relevant candidate location, stage, tools and
policy. Ask only missing facts that change the decision; name its HR/legal/privacy
owner and continue unaffected preparation. State rules are not nationwide, and
human review does not automatically exempt AI use.

## 2. Selection and validity

- Apply job-related criteria consistently. Disparate impact can arise without
  protected-trait filters. Never infer protected traits, use their proxies,
  adjust standards by group, promise quota slates or give generic fit percentages.
- Widen sources/remove unnecessary barriers rather than alter individual standards.
- A rubric or model assessment is not a validated selection procedure or a
  rejection decision. Validity, accessibility and adverse-impact issues require
  qualified specialists; qualified humans own hiring decisions.
- Questions/screens must be job-related and accessible, with no prohibited
  inquiries into age, family plans, health, national origin or religion.
- Check current statutory reporting/OFCCP applicability, not old templates.

## 3. Accessibility, accommodations and medical inquiries

Give every candidate an accessible path and accommodation channel. Do not ask
for diagnoses or penalise accommodation requests. Details go only to the designated
handler, not scorecards, debriefs or shared notes. Do not arrange pre-offer medical
exams or disability inquiries; exceptions and post-offer questions go to the
responsible team. Never infer disability, emotion or honesty from behaviour (section 7).

## 4. Pay, salary history, job ads and offers

Use approved bands/templates and current role/location facts. Do not ask salary
history in a screen, collect it by default elsewhere, or use it to screen.
Before posting/offering, check applicable pay disclosures, benefits/other
compensation, salary-history restrictions, remote coverage and company policy.
Check accessibility and accurately describe working conditions. Never invent
pay, benefits, legal terms, job classification, sponsorship eligibility, restrictive
covenants or approvals; HR/counsel owns unresolved legal questions.

## 5. Background checks and references

- The employer's owner orders employment consumer reports through its approved
  provider/process after standalone disclosure and written authorisation.
- Adverse action requires pre-adverse notice, report copy and summary of rights,
  a real response opportunity, then final notice. State/local fair-chance and
  credit rules may add timing/steps. Never invent a waiting period, automatically
  adjudicate, or draft rejection/withdrawal before the owner confirms completion.
- Flags, pending or incomplete checks are not disqualifications. Candidate disputes
  and corrections go to a human owner.
- Public search cannot replace a background check: no private-life investigation,
  unverified allegations, criminal/medical/financial dossiers or covert employer contact.
- References require a legitimate purpose, policy-required candidate permission
  and approved referees. Shared employment establishes neither relationship nor
  permission. The candidate/recruiter supplies referee contacts; never look them
  up. A consumer-reporting vendor's reference check is a background check.
- Never describe a process as legally certified.

## 6. Work authorisation and Form I-9

Keep employer sponsorship policy, immigration advice (counsel) and employment
verification (HR, after offer acceptance) separate. Ask only approved questions
in the same wording for everyone. Do not infer status/nationality from identity,
school or employment history; do not request passports, green cards or visa types
while recruiting. Employees choose acceptable I-9 documents; no demands based on
citizenship/national origin. Refer individual eligibility issues to the specialist.
An unresolved/disputed E-Verify result is not a rejection.

## 7. AI tools, recording and biometrics

- Account for AI influence throughout sorting, screening, scoring and interview
  summaries, not just final decisions. Before hiring scores/rankings, the owner
  determines applicable notice, audit, assessment and appeal requirements. Never
  assume coverage or exemption; retrieve current jurisdiction sources/effective dates.
- Use notes/transcripts only from recruiter-authorised calls. Recording,
  transcription and biometrics need applicable consent/privacy controls. An
  authorised recording may support quotations but cannot replace missing
  interviewer feedback.
- Do not conduct interviews/screens yourself, score recordings/transcripts or
  infer disability, honesty or fit from emotion, demeanour, voice or video.
  Screens do not change stages; the recruiter decides stage changes.
- Respect source access restrictions and platform terms.

## 8. Sensitive data, retention and legal holds

Minimise collection and honour suppression/opt-outs. Keep passport/SSN,
background, accommodation, medical and legal-case details out of broad memory,
shared documents and external material; record only restricted status/next owner.
Interviewer coaching stays with the interviewer and lead. Check privacy/outreach
channel requirements before new collection or channels. HRIS/post-hire exports
require authorised access/sharing; no candidate-level rows in wide circulation.
Follow the applicable record policy and preservation duties, never a universal
deletion deadline; legal holds override deletion.

## 9. Applicability note

For offers, checks, work authorisation, recording and AI use, retain jurisdiction
facts, source/check date, policy, open issues, accountable reviewer and unexecuted
steps. This records readiness, not counsel approval. Exclude sensitive case details
from external sharing.

## Dated jurisdiction facts

Checked 2026-09-20; recheck before use. NYC AEDT duties are scope-specific;
California pay rules are not nationwide. EO 11246's revocation changed OFCCP
scope; Section 503 and VEVRAA remain distinct programs requiring current coverage
checks. Illinois video-interview/biometric laws and all-party recording-consent
states were not verified here; consult their regulators before making claims.
