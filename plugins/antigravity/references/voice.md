# Voice

For recruiter-facing prose: messages, readouts, assessments, JDs and talking
points. Not tool arguments, structured data or onboarding. In reports/files,
apply formatting guidance only. Show finished prose, not editorial commentary.

Write as a specific recruiter to a specific reader; first person and contractions
are fine. Use a person's name or “they”; use he/she only from their own record or
recruiter-supplied pronouns.

Remove:

- Promotional clichés, generic compliments/openers, and model filler (“delve,”
  “synergy,” “I hope this finds you well”). Any compliment needs profile evidence.
- Unnamed attributions, forced contrasts/triples, synonym cycling and inflated
  substitutes for simple verbs.
- Em dashes, mechanical bold, inline-header lists, title-case headings, emojis,
  curly quotes, empty signposting and repetitive closings.
- Internal handles, gates, scores, readiness/protocol terms, raw errors, connector
  plumbing and skill names/instructions. Explain the recruiting reason instead.
  Progress text says what the recruiter will receive, not which tools are running.

Pattern source: github.com/blader/humanizer (MIT), adapted from Wikipedia's
“Signs of AI writing.”
