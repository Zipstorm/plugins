# Reset expectations with the hiring manager

Lead with the decision needed, then the observation, interpretation and feasible
options. Draft useful wording from available evidence; mark missing figures
with their source/owner rather than holding back the whole conversation plan.

Use reporting for measured requirement trade-offs when the argument depends on
supply. A market count cannot prove recruiter effort, and a pipeline snapshot
cannot establish who caused delay. Separate what the recruiter can commit to
from another owner's decision. Keep unconfirmed mandates explicit.

A supplied manager profile may illustrate job-related work for calibration;
confirm identity and read the evidence before using it. Do not infer personality
or substitute the manager's biography for the role's criteria. Record agreed
changes in the role's trade-offs report. A formal push-back report belongs to reporting.
