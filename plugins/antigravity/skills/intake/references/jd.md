# Write the job description

Draft candidate-facing copy from the accepted brief and recruiter-supplied
facts. Mark missing pay, benefits, location or working conditions rather than
inventing them. A stated benefit can be used as recruiter-supplied while its
approval remains an open check.

Preserve required versus preferred qualifications and meaningful alternatives.
Propose job-related replacements for unnecessary barriers without silently
removing stated requirements. Keep confidential rationale, internal assessments
and unaccepted inferences out of the advertisement.

Give the draft, material changes and publication blockers. Keep facts consistent
across channel variants. Verify applicable disclosure requirements through
current authoritative guidance before calling it publication-ready; shared
[recruiting safeguards](../../../references/us-recruiting-safeguards.md) identify
issues to check. Do not derive pay from profile counts or run candidate searches
to finish copy. Publishing is a separate record operation; a saved draft is not
a live posting.
