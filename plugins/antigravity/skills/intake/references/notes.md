# Reconcile notes and changing requirements

Read the role's overview and the supplied notes, transcript or selected message
thread.
A role planned in a trade-offs report has that report too.
If asked to retrieve a meeting, use its time and participants; clarify
multiple matches. Label pasted excerpts, recorder summaries and truncated reads
accurately. Limit message research to the requested role and source.

Extract decisions, proposals, rejected options and commitments separately,
with speaker, date and source reference. Check ambiguous names, numbers and
negations. A newer message or casual suggestion does not override an accepted
decision. Resolve material contradictions while preserving the original source.

Reuse answered questions. Carry explicit recruiter-confirmed decisions into the
role record's next version without asking again; transcript-only interpretations remain
proposed until accepted. Record open decisions with owners and commitments with
due dates. Use [criteria](../../../references/criteria.md) for before/after
changes and, for saving,
[plan a role](../../../references/report-building.md).

Preserve unaffected criteria and evidence. Mark earlier measurements and
assessments with their original scope; identify what needs rerunning rather
than claiming it is updated. Imported criteria from another role are proposals,
with attribution, not permission to copy its candidates or measurements.
If an explicit refresh finds no change, do not create a new version.
