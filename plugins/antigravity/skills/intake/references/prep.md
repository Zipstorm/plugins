# Prepare for the intake meeting

Give the recruiter a usable first draft: known role facts, the most consequential
open decisions, why each matters, and an agenda fitting the meeting. Mark
missing facts; do not replace the requested preparation with a questionnaire.
Use the available JD, the role's trade-offs report and supplied context before seeking more.

For a requested research packet, resolve the correct job and use relevant,
authorized ATS history, team context and role-specific messages. Explain why
past roles are comparable. Do not automatically scan unrelated channels or
collect private compensation/performance records. Keep source dates and gaps.

Distinguish business outcomes, evidence of doing the work, and searchable
vocabulary. Earlier hires and manager preferences suggest questions, not new
requirements. Use reporting for supply/trade-off evidence when requested or
necessary to support the decision; never invent a count or use supply as pay.

Calibration examples require actual profiles and a request for that exercise.
Label them as calibration and record reactions as proposed criteria changes.
Manager-facing material uses aggregate patterns and paraphrases, excluding
candidate names, scorecard excerpts, private messages and recruiter-only hunches.
Save supported content in the role's trade-offs report ([plan a role](../../../references/report-building.md)).

For requested context gathering, use the role's [documents](../../../references/connectors/documents.md),
[hiring-team conversation](../../../references/connectors/team-chat.md) or
[meeting notes](../../../references/connectors/meeting-notes.md). Start from
confirmed workspace sources; do not search every connected account.
