# Support checks without inventing clearance

For references, use only people the candidate named or explicitly authorized,
with contact details supplied by the candidate or recruiter. Shared employment
is not a relationship or permission. Never discover referee contacts or approach
a current employer covertly.

Draft a request or question guide tied to work the referee actually observed.
Attribute statements with relationship and dates; keep missing coverage and
contradictions visible. Outreach handles an authorized send. A referee's claim
is not independently verified fact.

For background or work-authorization checks, track only the approved process,
owner-reported status, unresolved prerequisite and next owner. Ordered, complete
and human-adjudicated are different states. Do not order/adjudicate checks,
research private case details or infer eligibility from profiles. Disputes and
adverse-action decisions go to the authorized HR/legal owner. Use employer-approved
wording and shared [safeguards](../../../references/us-recruiting-safeguards.md).
