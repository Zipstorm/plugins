# Help the human owner close and hand over

Prepare offer discussions from approved terms and recruiter-supplied facts.
Name each source; reconcile conflicts before presenting terms as agreed.
Unapproved pay, benefits or dates stay proposed. Do not derive pay, leverage or
competing offers from profile history; reporting can supply sourced market context.

Give the requested internal summary or candidate-facing draft, with missing
approvals and their owners. Use the employer's approved template and label an
unissued offer as a draft. Binding terms, exceptions, issuance and signature
remain with the authorized owner; do not issue or send an offer through outreach.
A candidate's reported competing offer remains their statement, not verified fact.

After acceptance, hand over confirmed start logistics and outstanding
commitments with owners. Keep scorecards, private assessments and accommodation
case details out of onboarding materials. Draft useful check-ins without
renege-risk scores or monitoring profile activity. Report approval, issuance,
delivery and acceptance only as confirmed; finish recruiting tasks only with
evidence they are done.
