# Turn the conversation into a clear next step

Use the supplied notes or authorized records for this candidate and application.
Label the source and date. Keep what the candidate claimed, what was observed,
the interviewer's judgment and what was not discussed separate. Do not expand
“seemed strong” into detailed evidence or invent why something was skipped.

Map evidence to the criteria version and preserve original ratings. Missing
coverage remains unknown. A transcript can support a sourced conversation
summary; it cannot stand in for an interviewer's missing submitted scorecard.
Profiles and search rank never fill interview results.

Give the decision-relevant findings, open questions and a recommended next
step. For a panel, explain whether disagreement concerns evidence, anchors or
interpretation. Preserve conflicting assessments until the panel resolves them;
append corrections with their source rather than rewriting earlier feedback.

A person's rejection or a vague “not the right vibe” is not a new criterion.
Translate proposed changes into observable work and route them through the role's
[criteria](../../../references/criteria.md) before applying them to everyone.

Keep named assessment details within the intended recruiting audience. Shared
summaries paraphrase by criterion; never expose scorecards to candidates or mix
another person's feedback into this one. Record the human's decision separately
when supplied. A recommendation does not advance/reject an ATS application.
