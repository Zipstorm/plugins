# Managed SeekOut campaigns

Use this when the recruiter's SeekOut outreach setup sends outreach to the
*SeekOut workspace*, or they ask for a SeekOut campaign. Use SeekOut for
workspace-owned sequences, larger candidate groups and ongoing campaign
management: reading, drafting, enrolling and controlling are all SeekOut
actions. “Set up an outreach sequence for this workspace” means
prepare the sequence; starting it is a separate action.

Read the exact workspace and campaign in SeekOut before editing. Use returned identifiers
for workspace, segment, campaign and sender; another chat or visible pane does
not choose the target. Reuse an existing draft when it is the requested one.
Read its full content and current revision, preserve unrelated steps/settings,
and re-read conflicts before applying the intended change.

## Prepare the sequence

Write the first message from the role and candidate evidence. Each follow-up
adds a useful detail, with a reasonable interval and a clear final touch.
Set the chosen sending account, time zone/window and stop conditions supported
by the campaign. Keep opt-outs and bounces suppressed; do not follow up after a
real reply. Do not invent daily limits or promise deliverability.

Save the draft in SeekOut and open it for review. A sending account must be integrated
and ready in SeekOut before launch; use the returned setup action if it is not. Do not
switch to a different sender to bypass that requirement.

## Add people and launch

Resolve candidates to their saved workspace identities and use SeekOut to
review the exact campaign membership. Adding candidates to a campaign-linked workspace segment
can feed that campaign automatically; adding a candidate alone does not prove enrollment.
Check membership before adding anyone again. Use the provided Add confirmation
when enrollment is needed. Enrollment does not start the sequence.

For an explicit Start, Pause or Resume request, prepare the campaign review and
show the required confirmation in SeekOut. Only that confirmation submits the
control. Re-read a stale review; never manufacture confirmation or submit from
another chat. A Pause may leave already in-flight sends to finish.

After uncertainty, check the original command's status rather than starting a
new command. Report draft saved, membership confirmed, activation confirmed and
actual sends separately. Read campaign outcomes from SeekOut; a command accepted
for processing does not prove that messages were sent or delivered.
