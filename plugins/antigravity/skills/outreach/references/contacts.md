# Find contact details

Use addresses already supplied or retrieved for the intended people. Never
guess an address or infer it from an “email available” indicator. Match identity
before using a contact, and respect opt-outs across addresses and methods.

Use SeekOut to look up only the requested contact type for SeekOut-returned
candidates or members of the chosen workspace. Referee details must come from the recruiter
or candidate; internal employees use the authorized directory. Follow shared
[contact rules](../../../references/contact-data.md).

A lookup spends contact credits; addresses already looked up are free. Get the
agreement SeekOut's lookup asks for once (for a large batch, the recruiter's
yes to how many people and the most credits it can spend), and never repeat a
cancelled lookup. Quote only the returned cost and balance. Missing contacts do not stop a draft.

Collect pending results from SeekOut through the original job reference. A lost
response is recovered with the original request identity, never a fresh paid
lookup. Report requested, returned, unavailable and still-pending people
accurately, with source/verification status where known. Do not claim a
partial batch is complete or spend again on an explicit “none found”.
