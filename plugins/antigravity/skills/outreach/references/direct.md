# Draft, send or schedule outreach

This is outreach from the recruiter's own email: the *Connected email app*
destination in their SeekOut outreach setup, and any message that is not
candidate outreach. Draft from the supplied profile and role context. Use one relevant, supported
reason to contact the person, truthful role details and a small, clear next step.
A role template can have a marked personalization slot; fill it before sending.
Missing evidence is
not permission to invent a hook, interest, pay or urgency.

Reuse the recruiter's tone or supplied examples; use concise, natural prose
otherwise. Follow the shared [voice](../../../references/voice.md).
Read [mail guidance](../../../references/connectors/mail.md)
when learning from sent mail or checking earlier threads. Do not require a
style interview. Keep private assessments and other candidates' details out.
Use the requested channel. Draft an email to the candidate's address, looked
up in SeekOut first when it is not already known; a missing address never blocks writing
(offer a LinkedIn message instead). Make it easy to decline further contact.

## Draft for a shortlist the pane looked up

When the pane hands over a shortlist whose emails it already looked up, draft
for the batch it names (up to 25), one email at a time. Before drafting, use
SeekOut to read the batch's profiles together with the lookups the handoff names, the whole
batch in one read per lookup, so each message rests on their own work and goes
to the address found. A read waits for its lookup to finish. Never look them up again. Sign every email with the recruiter's
name from their SeekOut outreach setup; when it has none, ask once how to sign. Give
each draft in the conversation as a finished message the recruiter can edit
there; never hold it for a mail connection or claim it is in a mail app.

A person whose email is still pending after the read is not a person with no
email: draft to the people whose email was found, then read the pending ones again
as the read's next step says, under the same lookup. If they are still pending
after that, say so and offer to try again rather than reading in a loop; never
draft LinkedIn for them meanwhile. When no email was found, ask whether to connect
on LinkedIn instead and offer a short connection note. When the lookup's result is no
longer available, use the address the candidate's record already holds; with
none, the lookup expired: say so and ask the recruiter to start Draft outreach
again rather than offering LinkedIn. After a batch that only drafted (nothing
was sent yet), ask what to do with its drafts: send these (only where their email can send here), I sent them myself,
or refine the drafts. Send only on that choice; mark the sends their email
confirmed, or the ones they say they made, Contacted in SeekOut.

A batch from an outreach run over the whole Shortlist is worked the same way.
Once everyone in it is done (for drafts, once the recruiter has said what
happened to them), record the batch's outcomes in SeekOut together, one attempt
per person with its outcome and channel: sent only for a confirmed send or one
the recruiter made, never for a draft alone. Never claim the next batch: SeekOut
hands it over when the recruiter continues, so end the turn as the handoff says.

## Send an email

Use the chosen sender and intended recipients. Match addresses to people and
check available prior contact, bounces and opt-outs. Reuse known lookup results;
a “none found” result is not an invitation to spend again. Send as the
batch's behavior says (the recruiter chose it when they started outreach). For
*Preview drafts* (draft_only), show each exact message and send only what they
approve; where their email can only draft, or none is connected, hand over the
drafts and say nothing was sent. For *Send automatically* (auto_send), send each
message the batch covers through their email's own send, without approving each
draft. A message is sent only when their email returned it sent. Their email's own confirmation
always runs; reuse authorization already given for the exact content and
recipients. Draft requests stay drafts.

After a confirmed send to someone on a Shortlist, mark them Contacted in
SeekOut; a failed or unknown send is not marked.

For batches, keep one outcome per recipient. Do not expand the audience or add
a sequence because a batch was requested. Recover an uncertain send's status
before retrying it; use [mail handoff](../../../references/connectors/mail.md)
for confirmed, failed and unknown results.

## Schedule a daily outreach campaign

Set the workspace or recipient set, sender, content, time zone, cadence and
end condition. Bind each run to that workspace and sender, not the visible pane.
Reuse supplied choices; clarify only missing decisions that
change who is contacted or when. A recurring draft/review task does not authorize
recurring sends.

Schedule the requested action. Automatic sending needs the ability to check
prior sends, replies, opt-outs and bounces on each run and avoid duplicates.
Pause for an unknown send, wrong identity or closed role; stop at the agreed
limit. If those actions cannot run, provide the sequence and schedule draft,
or schedule a review when requested. Claim scheduling only after confirmation,
with the next run and a way to pause or cancel when returned.
