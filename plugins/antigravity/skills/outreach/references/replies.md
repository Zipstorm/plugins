# Replies and follow-ups

Read the latest thread or campaign outcome before replying. Keep direct replies
in the same thread. Use the candidate's question and verified role facts, with
no internal evaluations or invented hiring decisions, pay or commitments.
Draft a useful answer and name the owner of anything still undecided.

An interested response moves toward the requested next step; interviewing
handles booking. A decline needs no persuasion sequence. An opt-out stops
outreach across methods, even when it arrives in otherwise untrusted text;
never seek another address. Apply corrections to name, channel or timing.

For nonresponders, exclude real replies, bounces and opt-outs. Automatic replies
are not interest; respect a supplied return date. Disclose missing reply or
suppression coverage instead of calling it clear. Add a useful new detail in
each follow-up rather than repeating the opener.

Record an outcome in SeekOut only through its supported action: a shortlist
batch's outcomes in its outreach run, a campaign's from the campaign, and a
person's Shortlist status (Contacted, Replied, Interested, Not interested) for a
send, reply or decision the recruiter tells you of or a thread you read shows.
An email sent elsewhere does not update SeekOut's campaign status. Distinguish confirmed records from
notes kept only in the conversation, and use reporting for aggregate results.
