# Track where each candidate stands

A candidate's status belongs to one workspace. The same person saved to two
roles has two statuses, and neither changes the other. Name the workspace with
every status you read or set.

## Read it from the workspace

Read the workspace in SeekOut. Each saved person comes back with their status
and their latest outreach outcome, and the workspace with its counts (how many
were contacted, replied, are interested). Answer from that read and give its
date. Do not rebuild a status from a mail thread, a calendar or an earlier
message in the conversation when the workspace can be read. The
[work log](../../../references/work-log.md) says what work lies behind a status
and which plugin holds the record.

For "who still needs a reply" or "who is interested", list the people with that
status and offer the step that fits: a follow-up or a reply is **outreach**, a
meeting is **interviewing**.

## Set it

Use the statuses SeekOut offers and no others. Set one when the recruiter says
it, or when a record you read shows it:

- **Contacted:** their email confirmed a send, or the recruiter says they sent
  it. Never for a draft.
- **Replied:** a reply from the candidate was read in the thread, or the
  recruiter reports one. An automatic reply is not a reply.
- **Interested / Not interested:** the candidate said so, or the recruiter
  decided. An opt-out is Not interested and stops outreach.
- **A later stage** (a booked meeting, interviews, an offer, a hire): set it
  when SeekOut offers a status for it. When it does not, leave the status as it
  is, say where that stage is recorded (the calendar event, the ATS
  application), and do not use another status to stand in for it.

Move a status forward on evidence. Move it back, or correct it, only when the
recruiter names the status. Set several people in one change when they share
the status, and report set, unchanged and failed from the result. A failed
status change never stops the work it followed; say it was not recorded.

Removing someone from a workspace is a separate, requested change. A candidate
who declined keeps their status and stays, so nobody contacts them again.

## One person, or one status, across roles

For "where does this person stand" across roles, or "who is interested across
my open roles": list the recruiter's workspaces, read the ones they named (all
of them only when they asked for all), and answer one row per workspace:
workspace, status, latest outreach, read date. Never merge the rows into one
status or add counts across workspaces into one figure without saying so.

Someone contacted in one role is not thereby contacted in another. Say that
they were, so the recruiter can decide whether a second message is welcome.

## The workspace and the ATS are two records

The workspace status is SeekOut's. An ATS stage belongs to an application in
the ATS. Setting one never updates the other. Asked for "pipeline status", use
the record the recruiter means: [the workspace](project-records.md) for the
people picked for a role, [the ATS](ats-read.md) for applications and stages.
When both are wanted, show them side by side, labeled, never as one total.
