# Keep the promises made to candidates

Use real commitments: a promised decision date, interview follow-up or sent
message. Show source/time, owner and next action. Missing timestamps are unknown,
not overdue; an application's last update is not a promise. Distinguish business
and calendar days, dependencies and candidate-requested delays. Prioritize the
commitment and consequence, not an invented candidate-importance score.

Read the intended workspace and application. Keep repeated process delays
visible without penalizing candidates. Outreach prepares requested messages
and handles sending; a task list is not a sent update.

For rejection, use a human decision final enough to communicate, with source
and date. Resolve contradictory application records. Use only authorized feedback;
without a reason, state the outcome without inventing another hire, future
opportunities or a promise to retain data. After substantial interviews, support
the relationship owner's conversation with talking points and a written follow-up.
Keep internal assessment notes out of candidate-facing text.

A disposition is a separate [record change](change-plans.md). Do not code a
reason nobody gave or claim a message changed the ATS. Background-check decisions
and notices stay with the authorized HR/legal owner under
[shared safeguards](../../../references/us-recruiting-safeguards.md); do not draft
a final rejection before that owner confirms the required process is complete.
