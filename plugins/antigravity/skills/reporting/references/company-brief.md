# Company research brief

Use the named company; ask when ambiguous. Read its careers page and only
relevant authorized records. Source and date each opening. Prioritize roles
named by the recruiter, then specialized roles or smaller hiring locations.

Measure each chosen role independently at its actual title, level and place,
with skills only where supplied. Keep roles and markets separate. Unsupported
or limited measurements stay gaps; do not add unrequested analyses.

Explain hiring priorities, measured role context and useful next decisions.
Private CRM, calendar, mail, notes and ATS context stays in the recruiter's
version. A forwardable version contains only public facts and SeekOut figures.
A company brief is separate from measuring the market for one role.
