# Company talent flow

Resolve the requested company and region. Measure company-wide flow; role
filters do not narrow it. Explain previous-employer patterns, alumni destinations
and sourcing implications in markdown.

Current employees are indexed people, not total headcount. Feeders cover all
current employees, not recent hires or verified direct moves. Alumni are
all-time; recent hires have under a year in their job; departures use the
returned calendar year. Never net these different windows or connect two other
companies. Keep self-employed/inactive buckets separate, missing top-list counts
unknown, and sampled markets estimated. Function breakdowns do not make the
whole flow function-specific. Profiles cannot establish layoffs.
