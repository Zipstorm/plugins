# Company landscape

Measure one supported industry category, optionally within a named place.
A narrower sector needs agreement before substituting a broader category.
Role-specific employer questions belong to a market overview instead.

Explain which employers hold the measured population and any decision-relevant
concentrations. Counts are indexed people currently at those companies, not
hiring, growth or total headcount. Preserve returned ranking and geographic
scope; an unresolved place cannot be presented as a local measurement. Never
sum companies into a total or label the largest employers the best sourcing
targets without separate evidence.
