# Hiring manager update

Scope the update to one requisition and the manager's decision. Use current
pipeline evidence and relevant dated events. Markdown should explain progress,
risks and next actions with owners and applicable dates.

SeekOut's snapshot samples people; its breakdowns count their applications,
possibly to other requisitions. Never divide breakdowns by the people sample.
Top lists are incomplete. Counts are all-time except month-applied buckets;
they do not establish transitions, time in stage or conversion. Keep requested
but unavailable figures explicit. For a specific stage, a scoped ATS search
may supply a people total alongside the sampled applications.
