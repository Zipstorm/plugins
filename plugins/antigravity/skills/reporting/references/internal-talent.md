# Internal talent

Measure the organization's employee skills or bench against the requested role
or capability. Use only supported internal measurements; public-profile counts
are not a substitute. Keep the employee population and observed skills clear.

Use markdown for capability coverage, gaps, development opportunities and next
steps. Recorded skills do not prove proficiency, readiness, interest or
availability for a move. Missing skill evidence is not proof that an employee
lacks it. Prefer aggregates, suppress identifying small groups and keep this
report private. Individual assessment belongs to interviewing with job-related
criteria.
