# Market comparison

A market overview that names several places (up to six): SeekOut measures the
report's baseline in each, under one role definition. Read each market's returned
totals, employer, title, skill and seniority facts; inspect the available fact
pages and gaps rather than assuming every facet is present. Add a single-market
profile only for evidence the comparison does not supply and the question needs.
Keep the hiring baseline and each comparison's country and work-model scope explicit. Cross-region
comparisons need the regions themselves named, not sample cities standing in
for them.

Use markdown to explain meaningful differences, constraints and implications for
where to recruit. Label estimates and unmeasured markets. Employer concentration
does not establish hiring demand, pay pressure or availability. Compare the
returned figures directly; never add overlapping markets into a total or infer
an unmeasured intersection from separate percentages.
