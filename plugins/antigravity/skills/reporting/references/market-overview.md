# Market overview

The report's baseline search is the as-written role and market, preserving
accepted titles, skills, levels and location. A market overview of that market
names no places; one place reads another market, and several are compared
([market comparison](market-comparison.md)). SeekOut measures it on the
baseline, so its makeup describes the same pool as the report's other modules.
Use its relevant employer and skill facets to explain feasibility and
constraints. Useful markdown holds findings, supported recommendations and the
next hiring decision.

Employer facets describe the current matching pool, not hiring, departures or
net movement. Missing top-list employers are unknown, not zero. Never reuse
hiring/exporter/neutral classifications from old reports. Keep verification
samples and candidate identities out of the report. A changed requirement
belongs in requirement trade-offs, not the baseline.
