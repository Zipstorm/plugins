# Push-back brief

Build a hiring-manager decision from the as-written pool (the report's
baseline) and its requirement trade-offs, each a variant relaxing one
requirement. Preserve non-negotiable anchors unless the recruiter puts them up
for discussion.

Write the supported constraint, options, recommendation and manager asks in
markdown. Keep private stakeholder observations and recruiter tactics out. Say when
no pushback is warranted. Pool counts cannot establish pay, commute willingness
or acceptance of a lower title. Keep dated ATS pipeline context separate from
market measurements; never combine them into conversion rates.
