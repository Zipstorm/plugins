# Talent map

Compare the role, defined once, across the hiring market and relevant
alternatives. The report's baseline search is the role in its hiring market;
the map names only the other places (up to 11), and SeekOut searches the
baseline in each, saves and measures it. Never search each market yourself.
A map compares at least two markets: when the recruiter names no other
market, leave the places empty and SeekOut adds the three nearest at the
baseline's own grain (city, state or country).
Preserve country, commute and work-model constraints; propose hubs only for an
exploratory request, and then at most six of your own choosing: the limit of
11 is for the markets the recruiter names.

Each market's count is its own search; never add markets into a total or
attribute one market's count to another. Write geographic findings and
sourcing recommendations in the body, and the market you recommend as the
first next step, citing its variant's report ref. Add a place to the map
rather than proposing a market it already holds. Keep the body complementary
to the map.
