# Their environment

For onboarding, follow the [walkthrough](walkthrough.md). Check the connection metadata relevant to the provider the recruiter named. Describe installation, sign-in and available actions separately. During setup, defer content reads, inbox searches and ATS job lookups until the recruiter requests a task requiring them. Distinguish SeekOut’s ATS connection from a host plugin for the same provider.

When a connection would help, follow the [client’s discovery flow](../../../references/connectors/README.md#finding-a-missing-connector) and its current eligibility and confirmation controls. Offer a supported connection when the client makes it available, and wait for the recruiter’s choice. Continue with the [starters](starter-prompts.md) if the connection is unavailable or declined. Keep credentials out of account notes.
