# Make it mine

Done means, in one reply where it can:

- **The request is restated** as a workflow they can check: what it reads, what
  it makes, when it runs.
- **A request another skill owns is routed,** not rebuilt: the owning skill
  takes it.
- **Anything else is done once, now,** with their real inputs, so they see it
  work.

## 1. Restate it

In one short paragraph: the trigger ("every Monday morning"), what it reads
(their open roles, past posts they point to), what it makes (a draft in their
voice).
Ask one question only if the answer changes what gets made.

## 2. Route what a skill owns

Before doing anything, check the request against the skills. If one owns it,
hand it over with the request in their words and stop here:

- measuring a pool, market or company, or a company brief: **reporting**;
- role criteria, intake or a JD: **intake**;
- finding people or off-limits employers: **sourcing**;
- candidate messages: **outreach**;
- candidate assessment, interviews, debriefs and closing: **interviewing**;
- saved people, statuses, exports or the req radar: **pipeline-management**;
- recruiting performance, analytics or digests: **reporting**;
- their tools and what is connected: [get started](get-started.md); something
  broken: [troubleshooting](../../../references/troubleshoot.md).

A file or update for another reader stays with the workflow that owns its content.

A standing change to how one of those works ("always leave out Acme", "PDFs in
our brand") is kept in the conversation, or wherever the client keeps their
preferences, and followed from then on.

## 3. Do it once

Follow [the house style](make-it-mine-house-style.md).

Close with the output and the restated workflow, so they can ask again the same
way.
