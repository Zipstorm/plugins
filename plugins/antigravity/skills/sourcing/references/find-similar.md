# Named people and lookalikes

For a specific person, use SeekOut's person match with their exact supplied
profile link or name, with
known professional anchors to disambiguate. Pass a profile link to SeekOut's
person match as given; never fetch the profile page yourself. A failed match stays unmatched;
ask for name/current employer rather than inventing an identity. Do not turn
a named-person lookup into a broad role search.

For “more like these people”, use SeekOut to read their profiles and use the job-related
signals the recruiter liked. A selection can establish the preference; ask
only when its meaning is unclear. State the useful shared signals without
inventing years, tools or employer restrictions. Keep the original search's
location, source, function, level and exclusions unless asked to change them.
Employer or school reputation alone is not evidence of fit.

People named incidentally in a JD need an explicit treatment: exemplar,
already in play/excluded, or neither. Do not silently filter on them.
An internal exemplar can inform a public search's job-related criteria, but
private employee information must not be copied into the public query or slate.
Use [refinement](refine.md) for subsequent feedback and confirm identity before
combining records across sources.
