# Employees and past applicants

Choose the requested source before building the SeekOut search: public
profiles, the organization's employees or the connected ATS's applicants. Public profiles are
not a substitute for private employee records or ATS applications. Rebuild
filters using that source's actual fields and returned values; public power
filters do not carry across. Never silently widen to another organization or
source.

## Employees

Search employees in SeekOut with the authorized organization, requested role/skills and department or manager
scope. Resolve internal skills and organization-specific values; do not guess
manager emails or substitute free text for an unmatched department. Distinguish
direct reports from the full reporting tree, and hire date from time in role.
Check against the total population to spot filters that did nothing. Read the
[internal catalogue](../../../references/packs/internal.md) when needed.

Keep private evidence separate from public claims. Skill overlap does not prove
mobility interest, readiness or eligibility. A bench-versus-market comparison
requires both sources and an explicit comparison request.

## Rediscovery

Search past applicants in SeekOut with the connected ATS's job, stage, status
and title vocabulary
([ATS notes](../../../references/packs/ats.md)). Keep people and applications
separate. Prior hire/reject labels start the investigation; they do not prove
fit for this role. Read prior application evidence alongside current evidence,
with dates, stale fields and contact restrictions visible.

With no suitable connection, use a supplied export or explain the missing
source. Search multiple pools only when requested or needed for the comparison;
report per-source counts and confirmed duplicates, never add overlapping pools.
