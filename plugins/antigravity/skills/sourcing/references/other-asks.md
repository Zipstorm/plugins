# Confidential searches, referrals and future pools

Keep confidential replacement names and internal rationale out of queries and
shared results. For executive searches, preserve the stated level and function;
prestige alone supplies no assessment evidence.

Use job-related criteria to broaden sourcing. Do not infer or target protected
traits through names, schools, employers or other proxies. Measure proposed
scope changes as [what-ifs](refine.md#what-ifs), preserving the baseline.

Shared employment or a team match does not establish a referral. Ask for the
actual relationship when an introduction is requested.

For a proactive pool, name the future need, source dates, relevance, owner and
refresh decision. Keep its search in a workspace. Offer recurring refreshes
only through an available [scheduler](../../../references/watches.md); do not
claim background monitoring. A named product workflow requires its actual
capability, not an imitation presented as that product's output.
